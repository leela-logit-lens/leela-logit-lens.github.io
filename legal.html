Privacy

Responsible for data processing on this page: Elias Sandmann (address above).

This page sets no cookies, uses no local storage and contains no analytics or tracking. Fonts and scripts are served from this site itself. Three services receive data from your browser:

  1. Hosting. The page is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you open it, GitHub processes your IP address and request data in server logs to deliver the page and keep the service secure. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  2. Playing against a layer. Only when you press on the play board or its controls does the page contact our policy server, hosted on Hugging Face Spaces (Hugging Face, Inc., USA). It sends the board position together with the game's previous positions (the network's input), the chosen layer and the legal moves, and receives that layer's move probabilities. Hugging Face processes your IP address to handle the request. Our server writes no request logs and does not store the positions; to limit how often it can be called, it keeps your IP address with a request counter in memory only, until the server restarts. See Hugging Face's privacy policy.
  3. Lichess. When the “Play against a layer” section comes into view, the page asks the Lichess API for the bots' online status and ratings, and repeats this every few minutes while the page is visible. Lichess (lichess.org, a French non-profit association) receives your IP address with these requests. Links to Lichess puzzles, bots and game analysis contact Lichess only when you click them. See the Lichess privacy policy.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: our legitimate interest in presenting the research and making its interactive parts work. GitHub and Hugging Face may process data in the USA.

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing of your data, and to object to its processing (Art. 15–18 and 21 GDPR). You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, such as the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information. Contact: the email address above.
